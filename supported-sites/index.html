Supported sites

MediaForge reads most public video and audio pages.

The download engine (yt-dlp) understands thousands of sites. MediaForge is for content you’re permitted to save, for example:

  • Your own uploads on video platforms.
  • Creative Commons and public-domain media: Internet Archive, NASA, Blender open movies, Wikimedia Commons.
  • Sites that offer downloads or allow saving in their terms.

Some media needs you to be signed in. With a Pro plan you can use your own signed-in session, where the site permits it; the session stays on your computer.

MediaForge doesn’t remove DRM, get around paywalls, or break access controls.
