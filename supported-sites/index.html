Supported sites

MediaForge’s download engine reads thousands of sites. These are the ones people ask about most, with an honest status for each. Only save content you’re permitted to save.

Global platforms

  • Yo

    YouTube

    Engine support

    Your own uploads and Creative Commons videos. The browser extension isn’t available on YouTube.

  • Vi

    Vimeo

    Sign-in needed

    Vimeo now asks for sign-in; use your own account’s session. Creators can also allow downloads.

  • Fa

    Facebook

    Sign-in needed

    Public videos and your own Pages’ videos; many need your session.

  • In

    Instagram

    Sign-in needed

    Your own posts and Reels with your session.

  • X

    X Twitter

    Engine support

    Videos in public posts.

  • Da

    Dailymotion

    Engine support

    Public videos.

  • Tw

    Twitch

    Engine support

    Clips and past broadcasts (VODs).

  • Re

    Reddit

    Engine support

    Videos hosted on Reddit.

  • So

    SoundCloud

    Engine support

    Tracks the artist has made downloadable or freely streamable.

What the statuses mean

Tested
We checked it with the current engine.
Engine support
The download engine can read it; not yet tested by us.
Sign-in needed
Works with your own signed-in session (Pro), where the site permits it.
Region-limited
Often only available from inside the site’s own region.
Not supported
DRM-protected. MediaForge never removes DRM.

Logos aren’t shown on purpose: MediaForge isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by any of these services, and their names are their owners’ trademarks. Cookies authenticate access; they don’t make downloading permitted. MediaForge never removes DRM or gets around paywalls.
