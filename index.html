How it works

  1. Step 1

    Paste or send a link

    Paste a link in the app, or send the page you’re on with the browser extension.

  2. Step 2

    Choose quality and format

    MediaForge shows what the source offers: resolution, audio, subtitles and an estimated size.

  3. Step 3

    Download and convert

    Downloads run in a queue with pause, resume and retry. Convert to MP4, MP3, FLAC and more.

  4. Step 4

    Find it later

    Everything lands in your library with its history: search, filter and open in one click.

Everything MediaForge does

All of it runs on your computer. Features marked for a plan are listed on the pricing page.

Download

  • Paste a link or drop it on the window; MediaForge reads what’s there in seconds
  • Every quality, frame rate, audio track and subtitle the source offers, with an estimated size
  • Video (MP4, WebM, MKV) or audio only (M4A, MP3, Opus)
  • Whole playlists: tick the items you want and they queue together
  • Subtitles in any available language, embedded or as separate files
  • “Remember for this site” saves your favourite format per site
  • File names from a template you choose, safe in Khmer, Chinese and French

Queue

  • Several downloads at once (1–10, by plan)
  • Pause, resume, cancel, retry and remove, one by one or in bulk
  • Picks up after a restart; interrupted downloads continue where they stopped
  • Live progress, speed and time left
  • Download window: only start downloads between times you set
  • Bandwidth limit, so downloads don’t take over your connection
  • Plain-language errors with a way forward: reconnect, retry, choose another format, change folder

Converter

  • Video to MP4, MKV or WebM; audio to MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV or FLAC
  • “Same as source” copies streams when it can: fast and lossless
  • Quality presets with plain explanations (High 1080p, Balanced 720p, Voice 96 kbps …)
  • Drop files from anywhere or send them from your library
  • Waits while your computer is busy, so it stays responsive

Library and history

  • Grid or list view, search, and filters for type, platform, date and playlist
  • Saved views, tags, and duplicate detection with “Show original”
  • Open a file or show it in its folder in one click; delete to the Trash with Undo
  • History records every step of every download: when, what changed and why
  • Export history as CSV or JSON

Browser extension

  • Chrome, Edge and Firefox
  • Finds the media on the page you’re on and sends it to MediaForge in one click
  • Several items on a page? Pick the ones you want
  • Never downloads in the browser and never touches cookies

Sessions and privacy

  • Use your own signed-in session (cookies.txt) for media that needs sign-in, where the site permits it
  • Sessions live in your system’s secure credential store, never in a file or on our server
  • Downloads, conversions, history and settings never leave your computer
  • No analytics unless you turn them on; logs hide cookies and tokens automatically

Plans and updates

  • Free, Basic, Pro and Lifetime plans; prices and limits shown live on this site
  • Licence key activation, with offline use for up to three weeks
  • Signed automatic updates (with the full release)

Made for everyone

  • English, ខ្មែរ (Khmer), 简体中文 and Français
  • Dark and light themes, full keyboard control, screen-reader labels and reduced motion
  • Windows (x64, Arm64), macOS (Apple silicon, Intel) and Linux

Questions

What can I download with MediaForge?
Content you’re permitted to save: your own uploads, Creative Commons and public-domain media, and sites that allow saving. MediaForge doesn’t remove DRM or get around paywalls or access controls.
Does MediaForge upload my files or history?
No. Downloads, conversions, history and signed-in sessions stay on your computer. Our server only handles licences, plan prices and app updates.
Which systems are supported?
Windows 10 and 11, macOS 12 or later (Apple silicon and Intel), and Linux (x64).
Can I try it for free?
Yes. The Free plan downloads up to 720p, one at a time, with conversion and history included.
